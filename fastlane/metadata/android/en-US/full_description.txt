Flashbyte lets you send and receive files directly between your devices — phone, laptop, or desktop — with no internet, no accounts, and no cloud uploads. Just pick a file, tap the device you want to send it to, and it transfers straight across your local network at full speed.

No sign-ups, no file size limits, no third parties involved. Everything stays on your local network.

• Direct device-to-device transfers over your local network
• No internet connection required
• No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking
• No file size limits — send files of any size
• All file types supported
• Cross-platform: Android, Linux, macOS, Windows, iOS
• Full encryption support
• Open source under MIT license
