Send and receive files directly between your devices, phone, laptop, or desktop, with no internet required, no accounts, and no cloud uploads.
No sign-ups, no file size limits, no third parties involved. Everything stays on your local network.

• Direct device-to-device transfers over your local network
• No internet connection required
• Transfer files over raw TCP (with TLS) for minimum overhead
• No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking
• All file types supported
• Cross-platform: Android, Linux, macOS, Windows, iOS
• Full encryption support
